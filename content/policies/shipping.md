## Dispatch

Every SPOOL product is printed after you order. Standard dispatch is 2-5 business days depending on the print time listed on the product page.

## Delivery

- Shipped across India via tracked courier.
- Free shipping on all orders.
- Tracking is sent on WhatsApp the moment the parcel leaves the studio.

## Damaged parcels

Report damage within 24 hours of delivery with photos of the parcel and the product. We reprint and reship at no cost.
