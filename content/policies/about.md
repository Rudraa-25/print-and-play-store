SPOOL is a precision 3D printing studio based in **Ahmedabad, Gujarat**, equipped with a fleet of high-precision FDM and SLA resin 3D printers.

We design, print, hand-finish, and pack everything right here in Ahmedabad. No warehouses, no bulk imports, no dropshipping. Every product receives a serial number and human quality inspection before it ships.

## How we work

- **Designed in Ahmedabad:** Prototyped and refined until performance meets perfection.
- **Printed to Order:** Zero waste manufacturing.
- **Hand Finished:** Meticulously inspected, hand-cleaned, and carefully packaged.
