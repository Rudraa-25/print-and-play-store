SPOOL is a one-room studio in Bengaluru with a wall of printers and too much filament.

We design, print, sand and pack everything ourselves. No warehouse, no bulk imports, no dropshipping. Every product gets a serial number and a human check before it goes in the box.

## How we work

- Design in-house, prototype until it stops annoying us.
- Print to order so nothing is wasted.
- Hand-finish, photograph, ship.

If a print line bothers you, tell us. We will reprint it.
