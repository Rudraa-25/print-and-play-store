## What material do you print in?

Mostly PLA and PETG. Each product page lists the materials it is printed in.

## How long until it ships?

Between 2 and 5 business days. The estimated print time and dispatch window are on every product page.

## Can I get a custom colour?

Often yes. Message us on WhatsApp before ordering and we will tell you what filament is in the studio.

## Will I see layer lines?

Yes, faintly. FDM printing builds objects in layers. We tune and hand-finish every piece, but layer lines are part of the craft.

## Do you accept returns?

No. Everything is made to order. Damaged or defective items are reprinted free if reported within 24 hours.

## How do I order?

Tick the acknowledgements on any product page and hit Order on WhatsApp. We confirm, print and ship.
