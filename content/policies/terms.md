## 1. 3D Printing & Fabrication Characteristics

All items sold on SPOOL Studio are **3D-printed to order** using high-precision FDM (Fused Deposition Modeling) and SLA (Resin) additive manufacturing technologies:
- **Layer Lines & Texture:** Micro layer lines (0.2mm / 0.12mm layer height) are an intrinsic characteristic of authentic 3D printed products and are not manufacturing defects.
- **Manufacturing Tolerances:** Standard 3D printing dimensional tolerances are **±0.2mm or ±0.5%** of overall geometry.
- **Color & Finish Variance:** Slight color hue or surface sheen variations may occur between filament spools or resin batches.

## 2. Material Specifications & Heat Limits

- **PLA (Polylactic Acid):** Heat deflection threshold is **55°C (131°F)**. Do not leave PLA parts inside parked hot vehicles or under direct harsh sunlight.
- **PETG (Polyethylene Terephthalate Glycol):** Heat deflection threshold is **75°C (167°F)**. Recommended for outdoor or automotive interior usage.
- **Mechanical Usage:** Products are intended for general consumer, desk, and decorative use unless explicitly specified for heavy structural load.

## 3. Made-to-Order & Cancellation Policy

- **Production Start:** Once an order is confirmed, fabrication begins immediately.
- **Non-Refundable:** Because items are custom 3D printed specifically to your chosen color and size parameters, orders cannot be cancelled or refunded once printing has commenced.

## 4. Transit Damage & Replacement Guarantee

- **Unboxing Video Requirement:** To claim a free replacement for items damaged during courier transit, you must record a clear, continuous unboxing video within **48 hours of delivery**.
- **Claims Process:** Send your order details along with the unboxing video to our WhatsApp support line (+91 93274 58583).

## 5. Custom CAD & Model Uploads

- **Intellectual Property:** By submitting a CAD model (.STL, .3MF, .STEP, .OBJ) for custom 3D printing, you warrant that you hold full ownership or valid licensing rights for the design.
- **Fabrication Liability:** SPOOL Studio is not liable for structural design flaws present in user-supplied CAD files.
