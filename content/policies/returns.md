## Returns are not accepted

SPOOL products are made to order. Nothing sits on a shelf; the printer starts after your order is confirmed. For that reason we cannot accept returns or exchanges for change of mind.

## What we do instead

- Damaged in transit? Reported within 24 hours, we reprint free.
- Manufacturing defect? We reprint free.
- Wrong item shipped? We reprint free and collect the wrong one.

Every customer confirms these terms with the checkboxes on the product page before ordering.
