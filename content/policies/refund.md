Because every item is printed to order, refunds are limited to the cases below.

## We refund when

- The parcel arrives damaged and is reported within 24 hours.
- The wrong product or wrong colour was shipped.
- The order cannot be printed or dispatched within 14 days.

## We do not refund

- Change of mind after printing has started.
- Minor colour variation between screen and print.
- Visible layer lines, which are a normal characteristic of FDM 3D printing.

Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5-7 business days of approval.
