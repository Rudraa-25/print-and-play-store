## What we collect

Your name, delivery address, phone number and email - only what is needed to print and deliver your order.

## How it is used

- Fulfilling and shipping orders.
- Order updates on WhatsApp or email.
- Nothing else. We do not sell or rent your data.

## Retention

Order records are kept for accounting purposes. Ask us any time to delete your contact details and we will.
